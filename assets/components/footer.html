Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services at Dream Makers Youth & Sports Club (DMYSC), we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Full Name, NIC Number, Date of Birth, and Address (e.g., No. 95/B, Dikhengama, Munagama).
  • Contact Details: Phone/WhatsApp number and Email address.
  • Membership Data: Profile pictures, team assignments, and payment records of monthly fees.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for club administration purposes:

  • To verify your identity and eligibility for membership.
  • To issue your Digital Member ID card.
  • To communicate club announcements, events, and match schedules.
  • To maintain transparency in fee collections and club funds.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We prioritize your privacy. We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers. Data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Access is restricted to authorized club administrators only.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data, please contact us at dreammakersysc@gmail.com or call us at +94 71 615 5666.
